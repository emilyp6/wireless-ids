What Is Wireless Intrusion Detection?

Wireless networks send data through the air, so anyone within range can listen in or transmit. That makes Wi-Fi easier to attack than wired networks.

An intrusion detection system (IDS) monitors network traffic and flags suspicious activity. The goal is to catch attacks early, before they disrupt users or steal data. AI helps by learning what normal traffic looks like and recognizing attack patterns automatically.

Conclusion

  • Each wireless attack changes measurable traffic features, which AI models can learn to recognize.
  • Rule-based methods are simple but miss attacks that stay under their thresholds.
  • Machine learning and deep learning are more accurate but need training data and are harder to explain.
